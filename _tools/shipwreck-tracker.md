Some of us play Dune: Awakening. When we're not busy shrieking like startled cultists as we run from sandworms, we can sometimes be found looting the shipwrecks that fall across Hagga Basin and the Deep Desert. This tracker maps every wreck we've looted: where it came down, and what fell out when we smacked it hard enough.

## What's on the map

- Every logged wreck, with what it dropped: hover a marker to see it, click to pin the details.
- Click anywhere to copy that position in the companion app's X/Y format.
- Lists of the schematics found and an aggregated hatch-loot table.
- A filter for wrecks from one game version.
- Patrol routes, including an efficient loop that passes within about half a kilometer of every known spawn spot.

Each map has its own help panel with the details.

## Your data

The maps store nothing about you. There's no sign-in.
