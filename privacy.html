Privacy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

We collect as little as we possibly can. You can play our games without an account and without handing us anything at all. This page is the whole picture — there's no fine print hiding behind it.

If you never sign in, we get nothing.

Our games run in your browser and save to your own device. Play without an account and nothing about you reaches us — no profile, no analytics trailing you around, nothing. (Your theme preference is remembered in your browser, but that never leaves it.)

Signing in is optional, and only for cloud saves.

The one reason to sign in is so your saves follow you from one device to the next. You sign in through Google or Discord, and we ask them for exactly one thing: a stable ID that lets us recognize you as the same person next time. We never receive your password, and we do not store your email address.

Worth knowing, because the two providers look different at the moment you sign in: Discord's consent screen may say it's sharing things like your username and avatar. That's Discord describing its own standard screen — not a list of what we keep. On our side, from either provider, the only thing that lands in our records is an opaque ID — something like discord: or google: followed by a number. It identifies you to us, and to no one else.

What we store, in full.

For a signed-in account, this is the complete list:

  • That opaque ID — the anonymous handle above.
  • Your save files for our games.
  • A sign-in session cookie, so you stay logged in between visits.

The cookie is HttpOnly, Secure, and same-site: it exists to keep you signed in, not to follow you around, and we don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Your saves live in Unspeakably Horrible Games cloud infrastructure and are used for exactly one thing — giving them back to you. Operating that service means our support staff can reach the stored records when they need to, and every such access is logged.

What we never do.

We don't run ads. We don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. We don't run analytics that identify you. There's no mailing list, because we never asked for your email and don't want it.

Deleting everything.

You can delete everything we hold — your saves and your ID — and gone means gone. Signed in, it's a single action; or email us and we'll do it for you, promptly and without making it weird.

Children.

Our games aren't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we'll remove it.

If this changes.

If we change how any of this works, we'll update this page and say so in our devlog — we won't quietly swap the terms out from under you. Questions about any of it: unspeakablyhorriblegames@gmail.com.
