Tithe

Our side projects and tools are free, and the servers behind them, and behind the online parts of our games, have to be fed whether anyone pays or not. If you'd like to help feed them, this is where you toss a few coins into the pit. Here's exactly what happens when you do.

What it pays for.

Our games and side projects run on cloud infrastructure: the servers, storage, and sign-in service behind cloud saves and sharing. Today that mostly fits inside free tiers. If enough of you show up, it won't, and the tithe is what covers the difference.

Where it goes.

Straight into Unspeakably Horrible Games' business account, not anyone's personal pocket. We're a company, not a charity, so a tithe isn't tax-deductible.

What you get.

Our gratitude, and the knowledge that the server minions are fed. A tithe doesn't buy anything: no games, no perks, no early access, no special treatment. The free things stay free either way.

What we see.

PayPal handles the payment, so we never see your card or bank details. PayPal does share your name and email address with us when you pay. We keep them only for our accounting records. They don't go on a mailing list (we don't have one), and we won't contact you unless it's about the payment itself.

Tithe through PayPal

Entirely optional. The Dark Ones see none of it.

Changed your mind?

Tithed by mistake, or regretting your offering? Within 30 days, email us or request a refund through PayPal, and we'll send it back, no questions asked.
