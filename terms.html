Terms

Last updated: August 29, 2026

The rules for using our games and this site — in plain language, and kept as short as we can make them. Playing our games means you're okay with what's here.

These are games in progress.

Our games are in active development. Things change, break, and get rebuilt, sometimes without much warning. We have reset save data during development, and while we're working to make that a thing of the past, you should treat any save as potentially temporary until a game leaves beta and we say so plainly.

Cloud saves are a convenience, not a guarantee.

When you're signed in, your saves are mirrored to our servers so they follow you between devices. That mirror is best-effort. The game saves to your own device first; the cloud copy sits on top of that as a convenience, and we can't promise it will always be there. We're not liable for lost or reset save data — so anything you'd truly hate to lose is worth keeping a copy of yourself.

Your account.

If you sign in, you do it through Google or Discord, and keeping that account secure is on you. We may suspend access that abuses the service or puts other players at risk.

Playing fair.

Don't ruin the games for other people, don't attack or probe our infrastructure, and don't use our games to do anything illegal. That's the whole acceptable-use policy.

Our games and this site are provided "as is," warts and all — this is early software about enormous horrors, after all. To the fullest extent the law allows, we're not liable for any damage or loss that comes from using them.
